## 1. Introduction

A quasi-static pressurized thin membrane is used to assess the volume-conservation
properties of the different immersed-boundary kernels. The membrane is
initialised in its circular equilibrium configuration with centre
$(1/2,\, 1/2)$ and radius $R = 1/4$, in a periodic unit square $[0,1]^2$ with
still background flow. The pressure difference across the boundary is balanced by
the membrane force, so any deviation of the enclosed area from its initial value
is a discretisation error of the coupling. The divergence-free composite
B-spline interpolation that the comparison revolves around is that of
{{< cite "gruninger2024local" "author" >}}, as applied to this case in
{{< cite "li2025local" "author" >}}.

### 2.1 Geometry

The membrane is a circle of radius $R = 1/4$ centred at $(1/2,\, 1/2)$ in the

The membrane force density is

$$
F(s,t) = \kappa\,\frac{\partial^2 X(s,t)}{\partial s^2} ,
$$

where $X$ are the Lagrangian coordinates, $s$ is the arclength parameter along
the membrane in its reference configuration, and the spring constant is
$\kappa = 1$.

Theoretically the vorticity should vanish identically throughout the domain
{{< cite "gruninger2024local" "author" >}}, so any non-zero vorticity is a
spurious velocity generated by the discrete force-spreading operator — the
quantity the kernel comparison reads.

### 2.3 Boundary and initial conditions

The domain is periodic and the fluid starts at rest. The membrane starts exactly
in its equilibrium configuration, so the pressure difference across it is
balanced by the membrane force from the first step on.

## 3. Numerical setup

The fluid grid is $128 \times 128$ ($h = 1/128$) with $\Delta t = h/8$ and
$\mathrm{MFAC} = 0.5$. A separate sensitivity study fixes $N = 128$ and varies
$\mathrm{MFAC} = 0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.25$ and $1.5$.

## 4. Results

### 4.1 Quantities of interest

The quantities of interest are the relative area change
$\Delta A(t) = \lvert A - A_0\rvert / \lvert A_0\rvert$ at each step, evaluated
from the tracer markers distributed along the immersed boundary, and the spurious
vorticity field, which should vanish identically.
